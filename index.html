Process

Simple steps. Clear results.

Every project follows the same path, so you always know what is being done, why it matters and what comes next.

  1. 01
    Audit

    Find the gaps

    We start with your business, your customers and your current search presence, so there is a clear baseline before any work begins.

    Details
    • Goals, services, locations and target customers
    • Technical audit: crawling, indexing, speed and mobile
    • Review of Google Business Profile, content and backlinks
    • Competitor and current ranking analysis
  2. 02
    Plan

    Pick priorities

    Findings become a plan. Every action is ranked by impact and effort, so the work that matters most comes first.

    Details
    • Keyword research and search intent mapping
    • Topical map and query clusters for semantic structure
    • Each target keyword assigned to one page
    • Clear KPIs: traffic, calls, leads and revenue
  3. 03
    Optimize

    Do the work

    Your site and profiles are improved so search engines and customers understand exactly what you offer and why to choose you.

    Details
    • Titles, descriptions, headings, URLs and internal links
    • Technical fixes, schema and page speed
    • Google Business Profile optimization
    • Content briefs for new and refreshed pages
  4. 04
    Authority

    Build trust

    Helpful content and relevant off-page work build the authority behind rankings, including visibility in AI search.

    Details
    • People-first service pages and articles
    • Content structured for AI Overviews and answer engines
    • Quality links, citations and brand mentions
    • Review strategy for local trust
  5. 05
    Report

    Show the numbers

    Results are tracked and the plan is refined from real data, so your SEO keeps improving instead of standing still.

    Details
    • Rankings, organic traffic, calls and conversions
    • Google Search Console and GA4 tracking
    • Monthly reports in plain language
    • Ongoing improvements based on what the data shows
